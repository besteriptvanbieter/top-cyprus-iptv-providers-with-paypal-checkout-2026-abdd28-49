What to Avoid: Risks of Other Provider Types

Generic Budget Resellers

These are cheap but unstable. Their servers are often overloaded, especially during popular live events. You may get heavy buffering or picture freezes every few minutes. Picture quality is usually SD or low HD. Activation can be delayed, and support is often slow email only. Many do not accept PayPal, and if they do, your order may not activate instantly.

Expat / International Server Lines

These providers focus on a wide range of channels from different countries. However, the servers are often located far from Cyprus, causing high latency. You might notice audio desync — the sound is not in sync with the picture. Live streams for sports or news can be unreliable. While some accept PayPal, activation is not instant. Support is usually limited to email.

VOD-Only Providers

These services are good for movies and series (video-on-demand). But their live TV streams are not stable. They often buffer or drop out during important moments. Many do not offer PayPal at all; they prefer cryptocurrency or bank transfer. If live sports or news matter to you, these providers are not a good fit.
